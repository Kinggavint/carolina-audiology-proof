Your first visit

No rush and no surprises. Here is what to bring and what happens when you come to see us in Rocky Mount.

Request an appointmentCall 252-977-4327

Before you come

  • Your insurance card and photo ID
  • A list of your medications
  • Any past hearing test results
  • Your hearing aids, if you wear them
  • A family member or friend, if you like
  • New patient forms: link or PDF to confirm

During your visit

  1. We listen. Tell us where hearing gets hard: church, restaurants, the TV, the grandkids. We also ask about your health, noise exposure and any ringing or dizziness.
  2. We look and test. We check your ears, then measure how you hear tones and speech in a quiet room.
  3. We explain. You see your results on a chart, explained in plain language, with time for questions.
  4. We plan together. If something can help, we lay out the options and prices. There is no need to decide on the spot.

Questions we hear

Plain answers about hearing care in Rocky Mount. Call us if yours is not here.

What happens at my first visit?

We talk about your hearing and health history, test your hearing, and explain the results in plain language with a plan. Family members are welcome to join you.

Do I need a referral to see an audiologist?

Many patients can book a hearing test directly. Some insurance plans ask for a referral before they pay, so call us and we can help you check what your plan needs.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes, and we encourage it. A familiar voice helps us understand how you hear the people you care about, and a second set of ears helps you remember the plan.
