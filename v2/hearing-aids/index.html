Which hearing aid brands are available?

Dr. Palmer's practice fits hearing aids from Widex, Starkey, Phonak, Signia and Oticon. Confirm the brands carried in Rocky Mount Working with several manufacturers means the recommendation can follow your hearing, your daily life and your budget.

Smartphone linked hearing aids

Hearing aids that connect to your smartphone let you control volume and settings from your phone, and deliver audio from your TV, phone calls and music straight to your hearing aids.

What styles of hearing aids are there?

  • Receiver in canal. Small and discreet behind the ear.
  • Behind the ear. Easy to handle, with room for more power.
  • In the ear and in the canal. Custom shells that sit in the ear.

What happens after my fitting?

Your brain needs a few weeks to adjust to new sound. Follow up visits fine tune your settings, teach you care and cleaning, and keep your hearing aids working for your life.

Cochlear implant evaluations

When hearing aids are no longer enough, Dr. Palmer offers cochlear implant evaluations to help you and your surgeon decide on next steps. Confirm this is offered in Rocky Mount

Paying for hearing aids

Coverage for hearing aids varies widely. Original Medicare does not cover them, but many Medicare Advantage and private plans include a benefit. See insurance and financing.

Questions we hear

Plain answers about hearing care in Rocky Mount. Call us if yours is not here.

How much do hearing aids cost?

Cost depends on the technology level and style that suit your hearing and your routine. Dr. Palmer explains your options and their prices before you decide, and we help you understand your insurance benefits and payment choices.

Can hearing aids connect to my phone?

Yes. Smartphone linked hearing aids let you adjust volume from your phone and stream phone calls, TV audio and music straight to your hearing aids.

Are over the counter hearing aids right for me?

Over the counter hearing aids are meant for adults with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss. A hearing test tells you whether your loss is in that range and whether a medical issue needs attention first.

Can you look at hearing aids I bought somewhere else?

Call us with the make and model and we will let you know how we can help.
